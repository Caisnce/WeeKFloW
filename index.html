How to use your planner

  1. Add tasks. Click a box under any day and type. Press Enter or the + button for another task, and Shift+Enter for a new line.
  2. Check them off. Tick the box next to a task when it's done. The × (or Backspace on an empty task) removes it.
  3. Set up your subjects. Click a subject name to rename it, use + Add subject for more rows, and pick your first date in "Week starts".
  4. Watch your score. Every day and subject shows its progress, and the week gets a percentage and a grade (A 90+, B 80+, C 70+, D 60+).
  5. Save your progress. Press Save & get code. The long code works on any device and the short one only in this browser. Paste either into Load by code to come back.
  6. Finish the week. Press Finish week to lock in your score and move the dates forward 7 days. You get a fresh code for the new week.
