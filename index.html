How to use your planner

  1. Week and Month. Click a box under any day and type. Enter or + adds another task, Shift+Enter makes a new line, and the box next to a task checks it off.
  2. Scores. Days, subjects, weeks and months show progress, and each page gets a percentage and a grade (A 90+, B 80+, C 70+, D 60+).
  3. Bonus game. On the Week page, press Bonus game and catch 15 points of stars in 20 seconds (bombs cost 2). A win adds +5% to this week's grade, once per week.
  4. Habits. Tick each day you did a habit. You get a streak, your best streak, and a 30-day completion percentage. Rename, add or remove habits any time.
  5. Focus. A Pomodoro timer with focus and break phases, a daily focus goal, and a distraction-free screen that hides everything else (Esc to leave).
  6. Theme. Press Theme for dark or light mode, an accent color, and a background: a preset, any color, or your own image.
  7. Save your progress. Press Save & get code. The long code works on any device and the short one only in this browser. Paste either into Load by code to come back. Theme and timer settings stay in this browser.
  8. Finish the week. Press Finish week to lock in your score and move the dates forward 7 days. You get a fresh code and a new bonus game.
